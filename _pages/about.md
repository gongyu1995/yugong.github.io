I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at Rutgers University, working under the supervision of [Prof. Bo Yuan](https://sites.google.com/site/boyuaneecs/). Before starting my Ph.D., I was a research assistant at the Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute, where I worked with [Prof. Li Jiang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=wCxFd8YAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and [Prof. Zhezhi He](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QzDf7GoAAAAJ&hl=en). I hold a Master’s degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and a Bachelor’s degree from Wuhan University of Technology.

My research focuses on several exciting areas, including:

* Efficient architecture/system and algorithm co-design for AI
* Design space exploration for AI deployment on scalable architecture
* System design with next-generation technologies such as CXL and ScaleUp Network

I am passionate about pushing the boundaries of AI system design and am always eager to explore new ideas and collaborations in this rapidly evolving field.
